## midnight

Asset: assets/pages-midnight.webp

Prompt:

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: illustrated open-book page background for a fairytale reader. Edit the attached open two-page book spread into the midnight color variant. The entire parchment on BOTH pages must become deep midnight-blue / ink-purple dyed vellum (#252538), with clear fine visible fibrous paper grain, softly worn edges, indigo marbling and subtle silver celestial linework. This is truly dark blue paper, not a gray dimmed original and not a dark rectangular text panel. Retain rich gold gilded borders, purple flowers and white lilies, bright moonlight, ornate purple leather outside edge. The empty writing areas should be uniformly dark enough for ivory text. Preserve the original two equal portrait pages side by side, central gutter exactly in the middle, original framing, book edges, ornament positions, puzzle outlines, lilies, books, tea cup, lantern, castles, wisteria and stars. Keep large EMPTY textured writing areas; NO lettering, NO text, NO added content, NO central rectangle, NO UI. Edge-to-edge landscape 3:2 composition matching reference. The two page halves must be equally sized and align at the central spine.
